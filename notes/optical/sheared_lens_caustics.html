Assumptions & domain of validity

  • γ is real and the shear axis is aligned with the real axis — a general complex shear γe^{2iφ_γ} can always be rotated into this form without loss of generality.
  • Lengths are in units of the Einstein radius (θ_E = 1) for both the image plane (z) and source plane (ζ).
  • Valid for |γ| < 1: a single closed critical curve with exactly four cusps — this is the standard point-mass-plus-external-shear configuration, sometimes called the Chang–Refsdal lens.
  • As γ → 1⁻ the vertical cusps diverge (∝ 1/√(1−γ)); γ = 1 is a genuine bifurcation of the topology and is out of scope here, so the slider stops at 0.95.
  • This models a point mass perturbed by uniform external shear — a standard idealization for a star perturbed by a distant companion, not a full multi-body or extended-mass treatment.
